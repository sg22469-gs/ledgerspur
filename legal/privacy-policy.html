Privacy Policy (CCPA & GDPR Compliant)

Last Updated: October 2026

At LedgerSpur (accessible from https://ledgerspur.com), one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by LedgerSpur and how we use it, in compliance with the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and standard US data privacy frameworks.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better financial insights and personalized experiences:

  • Log Files: Like most standard websites, LedgerSpur utilizes log files. These include internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser types, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and click counts. These are not linked to any personally identifiable information.
  • Cookies and Web Beacons: LedgerSpur uses cookies to store visitor preferences, optimize web pages, and customize user experiences based on browser characteristics.

2. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to LedgerSpur.com and other sites on the internet. Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Affiliate Partners

Some of our advertising and financial partners may use cookies and web beacons on our site. Our partners include major US financial networks and affiliate programs (such as Impact, Commission Junction, and Google AdSense). These third-party ad servers or ad networks use technology in their respective advertisements and links that appear on LedgerSpur, which are sent directly to your browser.

4. California Consumer Privacy Rights (CCPA Notice)

Under the CCPA, California consumers have the right to:

  • Request disclosure of categories and specific pieces of personal data collected.
  • Request deletion of personal data collected by the business.
  • Request that a business not sell the consumer's personal data (We do not sell personal data).
