Editorial Methodology & Fact-Checking

Every review, comparison, and rate guide published on LedgerSpur undergoes rigorous editorial review:

  1. Direct Product Testing: We open real checking accounts, test mobile banking apps, and evaluate customer service wait times.
  2. Rate Verification: APYs and interest calculations are cross-checked directly with official Federal Reserve updates and bank disclosures.
  3. FDIC / NCUA Verification: We confirm federal deposit insurance backing (up to $250,000 per depositor) before recommending any financial institution.
